# 🏕️ CampIn — Company Brief

**CampIn** (campin.co.in) is India's first community-driven, permission-first marketplace for outdoor stays, campsites, and overlanding stops.

---

## The Pitch
In a market where outdoor travel is surging but highly unorganized, CampIn connects private landowners (coffee estates, organic farms, hillside orchards, private terraces) with campers, vanlifers, and road trippers. CampIn replaces unsafe "wild camping" and overpriced glamping resorts with a verified, trust-gated, and accessible network of local outdoor stays.

---

## Core Problem We Solve
1. **Legal & Safety Concerns**: Wild camping on Indian public lands or reservoirs often leads to forest department fines or security risks. CampIn guarantees **100% legal, permissioned private land stays**.
2. **Invisible Supply**: Small-scale rural farmers and coffee growers have beautiful properties but lack the digital skills to attract urban travelers. CampIn provides them with free, easy listing tools.
3. **Vanlife Infrastructure**: The growing community of campervans and RV overlanders in India has zero verified places to park overnight, charge batteries, and refill clean water. CampIn is building the nation's first overlanding stop network.

---

## The Verification Promise
Every campsite listed on CampIn undergoes a strict 4-pillar audit before being approved:
1. **Landowner Permission**: Documented title checks to ensure stays are legal.
2. **Washroom Check**: Access to locked, clean sanitation.
3. **Drinking Water**: Verified availability of fresh, clean water.
4. **GPS Integrity**: Exact pinned coordinates to eliminate lost detours in low-signal rural zones.

---

## Quick Numbers (2026 Target)
* **Launch Regions**: Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and Meghalaya.
* **MVP Pilot**: 10+ verified campsites, 500+ camper waitlist members, and simulated email operations active.
* **Pricing Model**: Low-cost BYOT (Bring Your Own Tent) starting at ₹300–₹800/night per pitch, with pre-pitched and glamping tiers ranging from ₹1,200 to ₹4,000/night.
