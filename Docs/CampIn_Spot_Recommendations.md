# 📍 CampIn — Top Target Campsite Region Recommendations

These are the primary hubs where CampIn should focus its manual verification audits. These spots represent high-intent camper markets with an abundance of private landowners interested in passive tourism income.

---

## 1. The Coffee Estate Glades (Coorg & Chikmagalur)
* **State**: Karnataka
* **Best Season**: October to March (dry & cool), June to September (dramatic monsoon overlanding)
* **Experiences**: Camping directly under silver oak shade canopies, estate walks, bird watching, and farm-fresh filter coffee tasting.
* **Why it's a priority**: Close proximity to Bangalore (5–6 hours drive) makes it the highest-demand weekend getaway. Large private estate plots simplify legal boundary checking.

---

## 2. The Wayanad Tea Valleys
* **State**: Kerala
* **Best Season**: September to May
* **Experiences**: Hillside terrace pitches overlooking tea plantation valleys, close to bamboo rafting spots and mist-covered hills.
* **Why it's a priority**: Kerala has progressive agro-tourism policies, and Wayanad attracts travelers from both Bangalore and Calicut/Kochi.

---

## 3. The Rocky Monoliths (Ramanagara & Kanakapura)
* **State**: Karnataka
* **Best Season**: Year-round (best in winter evenings)
* **Experiences**: Star-gazing at the base of spectacular granite climbing walls, lake-facing BYOT camps, and easy 1-night weekend getaways.
* **Why it's a priority**: Under 90 minutes from Bangalore city, making it the perfect market for beginner campers wanting to test their tents for a single night before embarking on longer trips.

---

## 4. The Coastal Campervan Route (Gokarna, Udupi & Karwar)
* **State**: Karnataka
* **Best Season**: November to February
* **Experiences**: Safe overnight campervan halts with sea views, coconut grove parking, UPI power hooks, and beachside BYOT sites.
* **Why it's a priority**: Connects the major Bangalore-to-Goa highway traffic. A key corridor for overlanders and vanlife travelers looking to avoid expensive beach hotels.
