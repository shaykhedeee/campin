## Slide 3: What Campers Look For
* **Headline**: Simple Needs, Great Experience
* **The 4 Essentials**:
  1. **A Secure space**: Fenced farm borders, locked gates, and an on-site caretaker.
  2. **Restrooms**: Access to a clean, lockable toilet with running water.
  3. **Drinking Water**: Fresh well water or clean drinking water outlets.
  4. **Peace & Quiet**: A place to sit under the trees and enjoy the view.

## Slide 5: How It Works
* **Headline**: Step-by-Step Hosting
* **Workflow**:
  1. **List Your Space**: Register your farm for free, upload pictures of your land and washrooms, and set your own prices.
  2. **Accept Bookings**: Review camper details (name, email, ID) and choose who is allowed to stay.
  3. **Welcome Guests**: Guide campers to their designated pitch zone and show them the restrooms.
  4. **Get Paid**: CampIn transfers the booking fee directly to your bank account via UPI or bank transfer.

---

## Slide 6: Trust & Safety First
* **Headline**: You Keep Control of Your Property
* **Bullets**:
  - **Verified Campers only**: Every camper must sign up with verified contact details.
  - **Set Your Own Rules**: Define check-in times, quiet hours (e.g., no music after 10 PM), and fire safety guidelines.
  - **Review System**: Rate guests after they leave to maintain a high-quality community.
