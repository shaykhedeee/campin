# CampIn Indian regional photography attribution

All files in this directory are local WebP derivatives of the exact Commons file pages below. Every derivative was resized and converted to WebP; no image is presented as evidence of a particular campsite, host, amenity, availability, or verification stage. `*-1600.webp` is the primary source and `*-900.webp` is its responsive companion.

## Inspection

Each `*-1600.webp` output was opened after conversion and checked for non-blank pixels, intact aspect ratio, relevant Indian landscape subject matter, and sensible cropping. The first Cherrapunji transfer was incomplete and visibly corrupted; it was rejected, re-downloaded from the Wikimedia upload URL, reconverted, and inspected again. The six accepted primary images are landscape or portrait photographs of the attributed regional subjects above, with no stock previews, search thumbnails, hotlinks, or operator-supplied campsite photography.
